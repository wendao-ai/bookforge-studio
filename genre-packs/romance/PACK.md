# 言情小说创作范式

## Core Engine

情感节拍器 + 关系状态机

## Creative Law

本 Pack 规定 `言情小说` 项目的结构、记忆、质量度量和人审边界。共享六阶段流水线仍然适用，但每个阶段都必须读取本 Pack 的结构范式、读者画像和一致性规则。

## Required Human Decisions

- 确认该类型是否为 primary genre。
- 确认核心读者承诺。
- 确认 `author-decides-emotional-contract` 模式下列出的关键创作决策。

## Quality Focus

- `emotional_logic`
- `chemistry`
- `tension_curve`
- `trope_satisfaction`
- `hea_payoff`

## Specialist Agents

- `romance-genre-lead`: coordinates emotional contract and romance specialists
- `emotion-beat-designer`: designs target emotion beats
- `relationship-arc-architect`: maintains relationship state transitions
- `tension-curve-manager`: controls escalation, relief, and dark moment
- `character-chemistry-designer`: builds attraction and compatibility
- `trope-curator`: selects and refreshes tropes
- `pacing-rhythm-tuner`: tunes sweet, bitter, and plot rhythm
- `reader-emotion-simulator`: simulates romance reader reactions
