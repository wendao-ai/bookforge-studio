# 通用非虚构创作范式

## Core Engine

论证链 + 证据图 + 概念路径

## Creative Law

本 Pack 规定 `通用非虚构` 项目的结构、记忆、质量度量和人审边界。共享六阶段流水线仍然适用，但每个阶段都必须读取本 Pack 的结构范式、读者画像和一致性规则。

## Required Human Decisions

- 确认该类型是否为 primary genre。
- 确认核心读者承诺。
- 确认 `thesis-review` 模式下列出的关键创作决策。

## Quality Focus

- `thesis_clarity`
- `argument_rigor`
- `evidence_alignment`
- `concept_order`
- `reader_value`

## Specialist Agents

- `nonfiction-general-genre-lead`: coordinates nonfiction thesis and structure
- `argument-structure-architect`: builds argument chain
- `evidence-curator`: curates evidence
- `concept-introduction-designer`: orders concepts for readers
- `logic-consistency-checker`: checks logic consistency
- `nonfiction-reader-simulator`: simulates target nonfiction readers
- `counterargument-editor`: strengthens objections and replies
- `case-study-selector`: selects examples and cases
