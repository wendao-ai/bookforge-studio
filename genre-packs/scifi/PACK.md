# 科幻小说创作范式

## Core Engine

世界设定圣经 + 科学一致性引擎

## Creative Law

本 Pack 规定 `科幻小说` 项目的结构、记忆、质量度量和人审边界。共享六阶段流水线仍然适用，但每个阶段都必须读取本 Pack 的结构范式、读者画像和一致性规则。

## Required Human Decisions

- 确认该类型是否为 primary genre。
- 确认核心读者承诺。
- 确认 `author-decides-worldview` 模式下列出的关键创作决策。

## Quality Focus

- `world_coherence`
- `scientific_plausibility`
- `sense_of_wonder`
- `narrative_pacing`
- `character_depth`

## Specialist Agents

- `scifi-genre-lead`: coordinates science-fiction paradigm and pack specialists
- `worldbuilding-architect`: builds and maintains the world bible
- `science-consistency-checker`: checks laws, tech limits, and contradictions
- `sense-of-wonder-designer`: designs awe and revelation beats
- `tech-extrapolation-advisor`: labels technology confidence and plausibility
- `plot-weaver`: weaves plot pressure with world rules
- `character-arc-designer`: keeps character arcs tied to speculative stakes
- `scifi-reader-simulator`: simulates hard-scifi, setting, and general readers
