# Capability Asset Rules

## Mandatory Standards

- Assets must record origin project, genre, problem solved, reusable pattern, evidence of success, and limitations.
- Author preferences must distinguish stable taste from one-off project decisions.
- Genre assets belong under `capability-library/by-genre/<genre>/`.
- Cross-genre assets belong under `capability-library/cross-genre/`.
- Assets should be short enough to retrieve and reuse.

## Anti-Patterns

- Dumping full chapters into the capability library.
- Recording a pattern without saying when not to use it.
- Mixing author private preference with genre-general advice.
- Treating failed experiments as proven patterns.
