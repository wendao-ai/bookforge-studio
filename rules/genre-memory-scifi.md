# Scifi Genre Memory Rules

## Mandatory Standards

- World bible entries must define law, scope, limitation, narrative consequence, and confidence.
- Tech tree entries must define capability, cost, dependency, failure mode, and who can access it.
- Timeline and faction data must avoid contradictions with established drafts.
- New world laws or technology breakthroughs require author approval.
- Speculative science must be labeled separately from plausible extrapolation.

## Anti-Patterns

- Adding convenient powers to solve plot problems.
- Making technology available to characters without cost or limits.
- Changing physical laws after drafting without backtracking.
- Treating worldbuilding notes as optional lore instead of consistency state.
