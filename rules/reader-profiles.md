# Reader Profile Rules

## Mandatory Standards

- Each Pack must define at least three reader profiles.
- Profiles must include expectations, sensitivities, delight triggers, rejection triggers, and review focus.
- Profiles must be specific to genre and audience promise.
- Review skills must cite the profiles they simulate.
- Profiles should include at least one demanding or skeptical reader.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic personas that fit every genre.
- Only positive readers who never reject the book.
- Profiles with demographics but no reading expectations.
- Using reader profiles for factual authority when expert review is required.
